# Development stages

## Stage 0: construct and extraction validation

Stage 0 establishes a minimal end-to-end path across selected families and discriminant cases. It covers matched prompts, tested responses, judge requests, factual packet gating, structured extraction, deterministic validation, and a basic human-validation path.

Its purpose is to expose construct, schema, evidence, operational, and judge-validity failures early. Stage-0 outcomes remain pilot evidence.

## Pilot and validation

The later pilot completes 24 matched families and all comparator opportunity sets. It adds expert-stratified gold, cross-family judges, non-LLM extraction baselines, factual packets, known-groups analysis, MTMM evidence, discriminant ceilings, reliability estimates, precision gates, and held-out validation.

## Full infrastructure

The full stack locks PostgreSQL 16+, JSONB, optional pgvector, pandas, DuckDB, immutable provenance, dual-engine golden equality, analysis-run versioning, and release manifests after construct and extraction gates pass.

## Inference boundaries

- A measures spontaneous framing.
- B minus A measures category activation.
- C minus A measures instruction-induced competence.
- Closed-provider comparisons remain descriptive.
- Documented within-lineage checkpoints and randomized supervised-fine-tuning manipulations support causal analysis under their specified controls.
